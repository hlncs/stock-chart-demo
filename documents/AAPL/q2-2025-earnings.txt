AAPL Q2 2025 Earnings Summary
==============================

Apple reported record revenue of $94.8 billion for Q2 2025, beating analyst expectations of $92.1 billion.
iPhone revenue grew 6% year-over-year driven by strong demand in emerging markets.
Services revenue reached an all-time high of $26.3 billion, up 14% YoY — positive outlook for recurring income.
Gross margin expanded to 47.1%, reflecting strong pricing power and operational efficiency.
The company raised its quarterly dividend by 4% and announced a $110 billion share buyback program.
Management provided raised guidance for Q3 2025 with revenue expected between $95–98 billion.

Key risks: Ongoing regulatory scrutiny in the EU over App Store policies; supply chain concentration in China.
