NVDA Q1 FY2026 Earnings Summary
================================

NVIDIA reported record quarterly revenue of $44.1 billion for Q1 FY2026, up 69% year-over-year.
Data Center revenue reached $39.1 billion, driven by Blackwell GPU architecture demand from hyperscalers.
Gross margin declined slightly to 71.3% (vs 74.6% prior quarter) due to ramp-up costs for new Blackwell systems.
Management guided Q2 FY2026 revenue at approximately $45 billion (+/- 2%), above Wall Street expectations.
Strong demand pipeline with order backlog extending into 2026 — positive outlook for continued growth.

Risks: Export restrictions to China remain a headwind; potential supply constraints on CoWoS packaging.
Competition from AMD MI350 and custom silicon from Google/Amazon/Microsoft could erode long-term market share.
