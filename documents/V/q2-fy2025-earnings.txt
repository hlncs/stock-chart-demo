Visa Q2 FY2025 Earnings Summary
================================

Visa reported Q2 FY2025 net revenues of $9.6 billion, up 9% year-over-year, beating estimates of $9.3 billion.
Payments volume grew 8% YoY to $3.9 trillion; cross-border volume (ex intra-Europe) up 13%.
Processed transactions rose 9% to 57.3 billion for the quarter.
EPS of $2.76 beat consensus by $0.12; the company announced a 13% increase in quarterly dividend.
Buyback program remains active with $4.7 billion returned to shareholders this quarter.

Risks: Department of Justice antitrust lawsuit regarding debit card network practices remains pending.
Macroeconomic slowdown could reduce consumer spending volumes — bearish risk to payment volumes.
